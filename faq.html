Products & ingredients

What products are in the Sunehri range?

Our imaginary range includes Protein Lassi in Sweet, Masala, and Mango flavours, Farm Fresh Dahi, Mango Dahi, Soft Paneer, Desi Ghee, and Flavoured Milk. Browse the products page for the current project prices.

Are the nutrition values real?

No. The Protein Lassi nutrition table contains invented values for this college project. It is not a real food label or dietary advice.

Where does the farmers' milk come from?

Sunehri Dairy is an imaginary company. Our story celebrates farmers and Punjab's dairy culture, but we do not source, make, or sell real products.

Basket & checkout

How much is delivery?

The demo delivery fee is ₹40 on orders under ₹500. Delivery is shown as free for orders of ₹500 or more.

Is there a coupon code?

Enter PUNJAB10 in the basket to apply a 10% demo discount to the item subtotal.

Can I place a real order or make a payment?

No. This website is a student project with no backend or payment service. Checkout only creates an imaginary confirmation in your browser; no real order is sent.

About the project

Is Sunehri Dairy a real company?

No. The company, products, prices, address, and phone number are fictional and created for a Digital Marketing practical file.

How can I contact the project team?

Use the contact form on our contact page. It saves the demo message only in this browser.
