Our little story

Why Sunehri?

Because the best everyday things start close to home.

Sunehri Dairy is an imaginary brand inspired by Punjab's generous food culture and the farmers who make dairy part of daily life. We believe good food can be simple, thoughtful and made with care.

Our idea is to celebrate farmers' milk, honest nourishment and the joy of sharing. We are a student project, but the values behind our story are real: respect the people who grow and make our food, and waste a little less along the way.
